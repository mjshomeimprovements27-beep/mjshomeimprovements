About MJ's Home Improvement

Local crew · Full service · St. Paul, MN

Owner-operated in St. Paul

MJ's Home Improvement is a St. Paul–based, owner-operated company. We take care of the work that keeps a property looking sharp and holding up through Minnesota's seasons — lawn care, landscaping and cleanups, gutter and exterior maintenance, tree trimming and removal, snow removal, and general home improvement projects.

We keep it simple: clear quotes, reliable scheduling, and work we're proud to sign. Most of our business comes from referrals and repeat customers in the Twin Cities, and we'd like to keep it that way.

  • Local & owner-operated — St. Paul, serving the Twin Cities metro
  • One crew, full service — no juggling multiple contractors
  • Free quotes — honest pricing, no pressure
